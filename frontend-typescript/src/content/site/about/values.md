title: Our values

items:
  - title: Security, without making it your problem to think about
    body: "For many of the organisations we work with, protecting information is not an abstract compliance concern; it can be tied directly to the safety of the people and communities behind the work. We build with that understanding as the starting point: infrastructure you can inspect and self-host if you choose, and a standard of care for your data that matches the standard you'd expect for your own."
  - title: Transparency, because trust has to be built on something
    body: "We believe that honest financial transactions between funders and the organisations they fund are what genuine civic trust is built on. That belief runs through the platform itself: open-source code anyone can examine, and a habit of saying plainly when our own evidence is early."
  - title: Collaboration is the cornerstone of this sector
    body: Good work in this sector has never been done by one organisation acting alone, and the tools it uses shouldn't pretend otherwise. We build software that gives funders and grantees a shared record they can both trust, so relationships run on visibility instead of suspicion and duplicated effort.
  - title: Free where it matters most
    body: Grassroots organisations will never pay for Open Grant Flow, because the organisations with the least capacity to spare are the ones this platform exists to help. Larger institutions fund the infrastructure, and any surplus goes back into it, and eventually into other nonprofit technology.
