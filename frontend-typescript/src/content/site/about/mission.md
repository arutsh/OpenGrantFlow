> Grant administration should not consume the capacity that grants exist to create.

Open Grant Flow's mission is to build shared, transparent financial infrastructure that lets funders and grantees work from one honest record, safely held, instead of two disconnected sets of paperwork, so that trust between them grows and the time it used to cost is returned to the work it was meant to fund.
