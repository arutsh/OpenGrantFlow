We use a small number of third-party services to operate the hosted platform: Hetzner (infrastructure, EU), Mailjet and MailerSend (transactional email, EU), Anthropic (AI-assisted features, when you supply your own API key — this involves a transfer of data outside the EU/EEA to the US), and Grafana Cloud (operational monitoring). Each only receives the data needed to provide its service.
