title: Frequently asked questions
status: published
items:
  - title: Is the pilot really free?
    body: Yes. There is no charge during the three-month pilot, and no obligation to continue afterwards. Grassroots organisations will always have free access.
  - title: What will we need to do?
    body: Use the platform on real or sample grants, and share honest feedback and ideas for what would make it more useful.
  - title: Who is it for?
    body: Nonprofits, NGOs, foundations and institutional donors that manage grant budgets and financial reporting.
  - title: Where does our data live?
    body: See our [Security & data](/security) page, or ask us directly above.
  - title: Is Open Grant Flow free?
    body: Grassroots organisations will never pay for it. Larger institutions pay sustainable prices that keep the hosted platform running. The code is open source, so anyone can also self-host it for free.
  - title: Do I need to be a Pilot Partner to get a demo?
    body: No. Anyone can request a demo; tick the pilot box only if you'd like to be considered for the programme.
  - title: Do I have to use the AI features?
    body: No. AI assistance is optional, and you can bring your own provider key.
