items:
  - title: Is Open Grant Flow free?
    body: "Pricing isn't set yet. We're still in the early stages of working with our pilot partners, so our pricing structure is still taking shape. Our intention is for Open Grant Flow to be free for small, grassroots organisations, and piloting is free: pilot partners pay nothing. We also offer a hosted platform."
  - title: Do I need to be a Pilot Partner to get a demo?
    body: No. Anyone can request a demo; tick the pilot box only if you'd like to be considered for the programme.
  - title: Do I have to use the AI features?
    body: No. AI assistance is optional, and you can bring your own provider key.
  - title: How many people from my organisation can use Open Grant Flow?
    body: During the pilot phase, you can add as many users as your organisation needs.
  - title: How long does it take to get set up?
    body: Once your account is active, you're ready to go. You can start drafting budgets, sharing them with your funders and submitting reports straight away. Every pilot partner also gets a walkthrough of the dashboard, so your team knows its way around from day one.
  - title: What support do you offer?
    body: If something isn't working, you can report it from any page using the message icon in the top bar. The team is notified straight away, and we'll get onto it as quickly as we can. Pilot partners also get a walkthrough of the dashboard and work directly with our team. Round-the-clock help with using the platform is on our roadmap, and we'll develop it further once the pilot phase is complete.
  - title: Do my funders need to use Open Grant Flow too?
    body: No. You can use Open Grant Flow on your own to build budgets and prepare reports, then export them as a structured Excel file that's easy to copy into your funder's own format. However, it works best when your funders join too. They get direct oversight of your budgets and reports, and you work on them together in one place.
