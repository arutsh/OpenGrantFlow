In our exploratory survey of 18 nonprofits, 15 said donor reporting requirements differ between funders, and 11 estimated that donor-specific reporting adds one to three days each time. Our goal is to bring that down to hours.

*Early, directional evidence from a small survey. Not a representative sample of the sector.*
